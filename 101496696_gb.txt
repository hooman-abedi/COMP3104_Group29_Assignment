George Brown Polytechnic, formerly George Brown College, is a public post-secondary institution in Toronto, Ontario. It opened in 1967 and takes its name from George Brown, a Toronto politician and journalist who was one of the Fathers of Confederation.

The school operates across three main campuses in downtown Toronto: St. James, Casa Loma and Waterfront. It offers a wide range of programs in areas such as computer technology, business, hospitality, health sciences, design and construction, with credentials ranging from certificates to four-year degrees.
