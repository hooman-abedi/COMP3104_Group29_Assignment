George Brown College, now officially known as George Brown Polytechnic, is located in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Established in 1967, it is named after George Brown, the founder of The Globe newspaper and a Father of Confederation.

George Brown has three main campuses in the downtown core. The Casa Loma campus is home to technology and construction programs, the St. James campus focuses on business, hospitality and culinary arts, and the Waterfront campus hosts health sciences programs. The school offers certificates, diplomas, graduate certificates and degrees.
